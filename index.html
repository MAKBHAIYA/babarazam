Babar Azam

A Living Legend



Babar Azam is widely regarded as one of the finest and most elegant batsmen in modern international cricket. Known for his pristine technique, classic balance, and exquisite cover drives, the Lahore-born right-handed top-order batter has been a cornerstone of Pakistan’s batting lineup across all formats. Over his career, he has achieved significant milestones, including holding top positions in ICC batting rankings, setting records for swift run-scoring in limited-overs cricket, and leading the national team across formats.
